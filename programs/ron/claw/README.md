# Claw

An arcade claw machine seen straight on through the glass. It has a marquee with chasing bulbs, a chrome claw on a gantry, a heaped pile of video-toy prizes (CRT TVs, VHS tapes, DVDs, synths, LZX tokens, eyeballs, red hats and wizards), an acrylic prize chute, and the cabinet base with its prize-door window.

## Controls (v0.5.0)

| Control | Function |
|---|---|
| K1 Gantry X | claw left/right, with glide |
| K2 Depth | front / middle / back row: the claw gets smaller the further back it reaches |
| K3 Grip | how forgiving the grab is: low = the claw must be centred on the prize |
| K4 Speed | gantry and cable glide speed |
| K5 Cabinet | Classic, Neon, Jackpot, Vaporwave, Mint, Studio |
| K6 Rig Odds | with S11 on: the chance (0–100 %) that a grabbed prize slips out of the claw on the way up |
| P12 Cable | slide down to lower the claw, up to raise it |
| S7 Drop | flip to close / open the claw by hand. Close over a prize to grab it; open over the chute to win, or anywhere else to drop it back into the pile |
| S8 Mode | Manual / Auto: in Auto the machine plays itself, grabbing random prizes and dropping them in the chute |
| S9 Grab | Auto-close (default): the claw opens as it drops and closes by itself as soon as a prize is in reach. Manual: you close it with S7 |
| S11 Rigged | Honest / Rigged: rigged, a grabbed prize sometimes slips at a random height on the lift (odds on K6) and tumbles back into the pile |
| S10 Video | your input video on the back wall of the cabinet |

## How to play

1. Line the claw up over a prize with K1, and pick its row with K2.
2. Slide P12 down. With S9 on Auto-close the claw opens on the way down and grabs by itself when it reaches a prize; on Manual, flip S7 to grab.
3. Slide P12 back up, carry the prize to the chute on the right and flip S7 to drop it in. It lands in the prize hole below the chute. Let go early and it tumbles back into the pile.

The joystick on the cabinet front leans as the claw moves and rocks when you change rows.
