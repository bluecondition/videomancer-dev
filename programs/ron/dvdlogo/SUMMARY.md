# DVD Logo — Classic bouncing DVD-logo screensaver with palette-cycling color on each bounce and a corner-hit party

## What it does
Up to four "DVD VIDEO" logos (384×174 1-bit bitmap, shown at 0.5×–3×) drift diagonally across the active area, bouncing off the screen edges. Every wall hit (and every new collision, in Bounce) advances that logo's colour through an 8-entry palette. A **corner hit** — a wall bounce within 16 px of the perpendicular wall — sets off a ~2 s party where every logo strobes through the palette. The Overlap switch picks what happens between logos: **Bounce** — they collide and are pushed apart along the axis of least penetration, the lowest-index lit logo winning colour where they overlap — or **Mix** — they pass through each other and their colours mix additively where glyph pixels overlap. Only lit glyph pixels count; a logo's bounding box never occludes or mixes.

## How it works
Logo bitmap lives in `dvdlogo_logo_pkg.C_LOGO_ROM` (generated by `dvdlogo.py` from `dvd_logo_source.png`), inferred into ~17 iCE40 BRAMs and shared across all instances. The single ROM port is round-robined across the 4 logos (slot = h_count mod 4), streaming each logo's next 16-bit word into a per-logo even/odd double buffer 8 px ahead of the beam.

Size: the renderer works in logo space. One shared accumulator per axis holds h_count·step / v_count·step (step = 1/scale from a serial divider); each logo adds a per-frame offset −x·step from the serial multiplier, so scaling costs one add per logo per axis per pixel — the same as the unscaled delta it replaced. The prefetch uses a second H accumulator preloaded with 8·step; it stays correct down to 0.5× (a word then spans 8 screen px). Scale/step latch once per field; the knob is deadbanded and glided; SD fields cap at 1.25×.

Positions are 13.8 fixed point, so speed is continuous down to 0.125 px/frame. Physics runs once per field (guarded so a serrated analog vsync can't double-step): predict, clamp + wall bounce + near-wall flags, a serial pair engine (one pair per clock: deltas → abs → overlap/penetration → accumulate), commit. All per-frame products (speed × X/Y weight, palette × brightness for 4 logos × Y/U/V) share one free-running serial shift-add multiplier.

Per-pixel render: lit flags → gated per-logo palette offsets → pairwise sums / priority selects → additive-clamped mix (Y ≤ 768, legal chroma) or priority pick → re-bias → wet select + blanking gate. 10-clock latency in every mode. Palette is U/V-swapped for the hardware, 90% amplitude.

## Controls
- **K1 Angle** — travel direction, shallow to steep (splits P12's speed between X and Y)
- **K2 Size** — logo size 50–300% (glided; SD capped at 125%)
- **K3 Count** — number of active logos (1–4)
- **K4 Clr Step** — colour advance per bounce (1–7, or Random)
- **K5 Tint** — rotates the 8-colour palette
- **K6 Bright** — logo brightness (25–100%), both colour modes
- **S7 Color** — Mono (palette luma only) / Palette
- **S8 Matte** — over a Black BG the logo is a window onto the video; over a Video BG it knocks a black hole in it
- **S9 BG** — Video / Black background
- **S10 Pause** — freeze position + colour
- **S11 Overlap** — Bounce (logos collide) vs Mix (pass through, colours add)
- **P12 Speed** — master speed, exponential 0.125–32 px/frame

## Presets
- Classic DVD
- Fast Chaos — random colour steps, high speed
- Lone Drifter — one logo, slow
- Glass Mix — logos pass through and mix
- Jumbo — two 3× logos
