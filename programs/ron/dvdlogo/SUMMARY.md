# DVD Logo — Classic bouncing DVD-logo screensaver with palette-cycling color on each wall bounce

## What it does
Up to four "DVD VIDEO" logos (384×174, 1-bit) drift diagonally across the active area, bouncing off the screen edges. The Overlap switch picks what happens between logos: **Bounce** — they collide off each other (classic), with the lowest-index lit logo winning color where they visually overlap — or **Mix** — they pass through each other and their colors mix additively where glyph pixels overlap. Only lit glyph pixels count; the black rectangle of a logo's bounding box never occludes or mixes. Every wall hit (and collision, in Bounce) advances that logo's color through an 8-entry palette. Modes include mono (single brightness) or palette, Matte to cut the logo shape through to underlying video, and Pause to freeze motion.

## How it works
Logo bitmap lives in `dvdlogo_logo_pkg.C_LOGO_ROM` (generated by `dvdlogo.py` from `dvd_logo_source.png`), inferred into ~17 iCE40 BRAMs and shared across all instances. The single ROM port is round-robined across the 4 logos (slot = h_count mod 4), streaming each logo's next 16-bit word into a per-logo even/odd double buffer 8 px ahead of the beam — so every logo has its own bitmap bit at every pixel. Physics runs once per vsync as a 4-phase FSM: predict, clamp + wall-bounce, pairwise AABB collision (disabled in Mix), commit + color advance. Per-pixel render is a 9-stage pipeline: lit flags → gated per-frame palette contributions → pairwise sums / priority selects → additive-clamped mix or priority pick, then a 4-stage `interpolator_u` for the final wet/dry mix.

## Controls
- **K1 X Spd** — horizontal velocity magnitude (1..16 px/frame)
- **K2 Y Spd** — vertical velocity magnitude (1..16 px/frame)
- **K3 Count** — number of active logos (1, 2, 3, or 4)
- **K4 Clr Step** — color advance per bounce (1..8 steps in the palette)
- **K5 Tint** — rotates the 8-color palette
- **K6 Bright** — luma level in Mono mode
- **T7 Color** — Mono / Palette
- **T8 Matte** — cut logo through to underlying video
- **T9 BG** — Video / Black background
- **T10 Pause** — freeze position + color
- **T11 Overlap** — Bounce (logos collide) vs Mix (pass through, colors mix additively)
- **Slider Mix** — wet/dry interpolator
