# DVD Logo — Classic bouncing DVD-logo screensaver with palette-cycling color on each wall bounce

## What it does
Up to four "DVD VIDEO" logos (384×174, 1-bit) drift diagonally across the active area, bouncing off the screen edges and off each other. Every wall hit and every logo-on-logo collision advances that logo's color through an 8-entry palette — the classic "did it hit the corner?" arcade. Modes include mono (single brightness) or palette, Matte to cut the logo shape through to underlying video, and Pause / Hold to freeze motion or color.

## How it works
Logo bitmap lives in `dvdlogo_logo_pkg.C_LOGO_ROM` (generated by `dvdlogo.py` from `dvd_logo_source.png`), inferred into ~17 iCE40 BRAMs and shared across all instances. Physics runs once per vsync as a 3-phase FSM: predict + wall-bounce, pairwise AABB collision (6 pairs for N=4), then commit positions and advance colors. Per-pixel render is a 9-stage pipeline with priority encoding (lowest-index logo wins on overlap), single-port BRAM read, and a 4-stage `interpolator_u` for the final wet/dry mix.

## Controls
- **K1 X Spd** — horizontal velocity magnitude (1..16 px/frame)
- **K2 Y Spd** — vertical velocity magnitude (1..16 px/frame)
- **K3 Count** — number of active logos (1, 2, 3, or 4)
- **K4 Clr Step** — color advance per bounce (1..8 steps in the palette)
- **K5 Tint** — rotates the 8-color palette
- **K6 Bright** — luma level in Mono mode
- **T7 Color** — Mono / Palette
- **T8 Matte** — cut logo through to underlying video
- **T9 BG** — Video / Black background
- **T10 Pause** — freeze position + color
- **T11 Hold** — Cycle vs Hold (suppress color advance on bounce)
- **Slider Mix** — wet/dry interpolator
