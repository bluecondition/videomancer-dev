# GILT v2: style spec (Phase 1)

Measured from 11 public-domain reproductions (`refs/SOURCES.md`) by the scripts in
`analysis/`. Machine-readable values: `style_constants.json` (generated by
`analysis/build_spec.py`; the single source of truth for the model and the VHDL). All
tables: `analysis/results/tables.md`.

**Status: zones approved (Ron, 2026-10-10).** Per-zone numbers use the polygons in
`analysis/zones.json`, drawn on The Kiss, Adele I and Fulfillment
(overlay: `analysis/figs/zones_sheet.png`). The section 10 decisions are still open.

## Conventions

- **Colour**: sRGB is the source of truth. CIELAB (D65) is used for analysis.
  Y10/Cb10/Cr10 = BT.709 10-bit legal range, the convention of the prompt's §3.2. These
  are for reporting only; hardware constants are generated in Phase 4.
- **Scale**: "px1080" = pixels when the painting's short side is shown as 1080 video
  lines. A square canvas is then 1080 px wide, and a Stoclet cartoon panel 1080 px wide.
  All texture is measured at this scale, because grain and flecks depend on resolution.
  At 2048 px the Kiss field's grain read twice as strong as at 1080.
- **Pixel families** (`analysis/lab.py: classify`):
  - black: L* < 20
  - gold: hue 40–105°, C* ≥ 12, plus the dark browns
  - green: hue 105–175°
  - red: hue < 40° or ≥ 330°, plus vermilion at hue 40–58° with C* ≥ 40. Measured
    bimodal: enamel reds peak at 40–50°, golds at 65–90°, Adele's orange gold included.
  - violet: hue 280–330°
  - blue: hue 175–280°
  - neutral: the remainder, at low chroma

- **Zones** (The Kiss / Adele I):
  - field: 46% / 33% of canvas
  - robe: 28% / 59%
  - meadow or floor: 20% / 2.5%
  - **flesh: only 3.7% / 3.3%**
  - hair: 1.9% / 1.4%
- **Jewels in the ornament**: Kiss robe red + violet + blue = 4.0% of the robe ≈ **1.1% of

  most colourful references (Hope II, the Fulfillment cartoon's red ground) reach 6–7%.
  This confirms a budget of about 1% default and about 7% max.
- **Black inside the robe**: 2.7–3.4% of robe area. The largest black masses are hair

## 4. Two golds: real, but the direction depends on the painting

| robe − field | ΔY10 | ΔCb10 | ΔCr10 | ΔC* | Δhue | field Y10 p10–p90 | robe Y10 p10–p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Kiss | **+177** | −68 | +16 | +18 | +3° | 359–589 | 496–719 |
| Adele I | +32 | +38 | −29 | **−10** | +6° | 394–576 | 364–671 |

- **Kiss**: a dull, dark brass ground with a bright, saturated figure gold. The step is
  bigger than the prompt's §3.2 (+130 Y / −55 Cb).
- **Adele**: the reverse on saturation. Her *field* is the most saturated gold in the set
  (C* 57, orange). Her ornament gold is paler and yellower, and its depth cue is
  *contrast*: a wider luma spread with more highlights and darker darks, not a brighter
  median.
- What both share: the robe's luma range is wider than the field's, and the two golds
  differ in hue by 3–6°.

| Fulfillment cartoon | 24 | 54 | 8.7 | 38 | 2.5 / 8.0 px | 7% |

- The real fields are much busier at pixel scale than the prompt's §5.8 assumed (±10–15
  Y codes for NEBULA). They are a dense, static gold dust.
- The Kiss is finer and sparkier, on a darker brass. Adele is smoother, with larger
  mottled blotches on a saturated orange gold. That is the opposite of §3.2's
  "Adele glitters, the Kiss is smooth".
- Silver flecks essentially don't occur in either.
- Caveat: these numbers depend on the reproduction. The Kiss copy is a very sharp Google
  Art Project scan; the Adele copy is softer.

## 7. Cells

Automatic segmentation counts cells that *contrast* with the leaf around them.
Gold-on-gold cells, visible only by engraved lines, are not counted. Check figures:
`analysis/figs/cells_*.png`.

| region | n | eq. diameter, % of robe width p10 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 90 / 99 | eq. diam px1080 p10 / 50 / 90 | aspect h/w p10 / 50 / 90 | σ ln |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kiss, man's robe | 129 | 1.0 / 1.3 / 2.0 / 3.0 / 4.2 / 10.3 | 5 / 11 / 23 | 0.9 / **1.75 / 3.5** | 0.58 |
| Kiss, woman's dress | 154 | 0.9 / 1.2 / 1.9 / 3.4 / 4.4 / 6.0 | 5 / 10 / 23 | 0.6 / 0.9 / 1.6 | 0.61 |
| Fulfillment ovals | 112 | **2.0 / 2.8 / 4.3 / 11.3 / 17.9 / 26.7** | 16 / 34 / 143 | 0.5 / 0.9 / 1.4 | 0.86 |
| Fulfillment checker block | 158 | 0.9 / 1.0 / 1.4 / 2.3 / 3.8 / 7.6 | 7 / 11 / 30 | 0.5 / 1.2 / 2.6 | 0.58 |

- Fulfillment independently reproduces the prompt's §3.2 distribution (2.2 / 3.0 / 4.8 /
  8.4 / 15.8 / 33): log-normal, many small cells, rare giants.
- **The banks differ.** The Kiss's cells are small, tight (σ ≈ 0.6) and tall: bars about
  11–15 × 42–64 px, aspect up to 3.5. Stoclet's are large and wide-spread (σ ≈ 0.86), ovals
  wider than tall.
- **Adele, measured by hand** (her outlines don't close, so automatic segmentation fails;
  `analysis/manual_measurements.json`):
  - almond eyes about 77 × 20 px (aspect 0.27), each crowning a gold triangle about
    62 × 28
  - dark and cobalt bars 10 × 44 (aspect 4.5)
  - a dense ground of silver and umber triangles 15–22 px across, about 13 per 100×100 px
- Aspect therefore needs to reach **about 4.5** for bars; §5.4 says 0.5–2.5.
- **Readability**: the Kiss's median cell is about 11 px at 1080, right at the
  prompt's 10–14 px floor.

## 8. Rims and outlines

The references do **not** support "every robe cell gets a pale-gold rim" (§5.7):
- Kiss black bars: no rim, flat umber on leaf.
- Kiss silver tablets: a black border 15–18% of the tablet's width (about 3 px).
- Ring-discs in the Kiss and Stoclet: thick rings, 25–35% of the diameter, round a
  contrasting centre.
- Fulfillment ovals (cartoon): a fine dark outline of about 1 px, with concentric
  engraved rings every 9–12 px inside.
- Adele's eyes: a dark outline of about 2 px.
- Gold-on-gold spirals: slightly darker gold lines, 2–3 px.
- Gold cloisonné outlines belong to the *executed* Stoclet mosaic, which is not in these
  references.

## 10. What this changes in the prompt (for Ron's decision)

1. **Two golds**: keep two golds, but the robe/field relation differs by bank. KISS =
   bright, saturated figure gold over dim brass. ADELE = paler, higher-contrast ornament
   over a saturated orange field.
2. **Field texture**: NEBULA and LEAF should carry about 2–3× the grain and many more
   flecks than §5.8 (σ ≈ 27–35 Y10, about 6% fleck pixels, 1.6–3.6 px, gold not silver).
   The Kiss is the finer, sparkier one.
3. **Calm field**: true for the Kiss and Adele. Stoclet fills its field with spirals.
   Should the ADELE-STOCLET bank allow sparse field spirals, or keep rule 2 strict?
4. **Rims**: make them dark, about 1–3 px, or absent (bars); tablets get black borders;
   discs are rings. Pale-gold cloisonné rims have no support in these references. Use
   them only where wanted, for example on Stoclet's large cells.
5. **Cell statistics per bank**: KISS small and tall (median about 2% of robe width,
   σ ln 0.6, aspect to 3.5–4.5). STOCLET large and wide-spread (median 4.3%, σ ln 0.86).
   ADELE mid-size eyes and triangles over dense small triangles.
6. **Composition targets**: flesh about 2–4% of the frame, robe 30–60%, field 30–45%.
   Jewels about 1% of canvas by default (about 4% of the robe), about 7% max.
   Black 1.5–3%.
