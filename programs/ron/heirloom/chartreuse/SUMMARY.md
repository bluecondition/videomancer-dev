# Chartreuse — a glossy lime surface folded into a sphere and crescents

**v0.6.1 (2026-11-02, phase 2): built, NOT flashed.** Heirloom #51, rebuilt from the photo
`photos/WIN_20231211_00_38_58_Pro.jpg`. A monochrome lime surface (dark green -> lime -> white),
mirrored about a horizontal axis 460 lines down. A big soft sphere sits on the left, then nested ")"
crescents sweep right (an hourglass neck, a white C). Their arms bend into zig-zag fins at
the top and bottom. Every band is shaded like chrome and outlined by thin white lines. The presets
are hand-written placeholders.

## Reading of the photo (measured)
Working files are in `.heirloom_work/rb_chartreuse/` (notes.md has the full log).
- **One colour ramp.** Every pixel lies on one 1-D curve:
  - G 76 -> 140 with R = B = 0;
  - then R rises at 1.6x G until G saturates at 245 (167, 244, 50);
  - then R and B rise to (245, 246, 236).

  The curve is measured channel by channel and carried as a 256-entry palette (arc length t).
  The photo's top-of-ramp chroma is pale lime: Cb -48 at Y 225-235, Cb -16 above 235, Cr ~0
  (hue ~-174 .. 179 deg).
- **Mirror.** Flip correlation 0.95-1.00 on the left, 0.57-0.86 at |Y| > 240 and on the right. The
  axis that best overlays the line network is y = 460.
- **Lines.** FWHM 7..24 px. The median line profile in scan order has a dark notch (G -30) ~7 px
  before the peak, a sharp rise over 5 px and a slow ~10 px tail after it. Horizontal lines get
  the notch too, so it is a level feature, not an x-delay. Broad cream gloss ridges are NOT
  lines: only ridges <= 26 px wide on the B channel count.
- **No oscillator field explains the frame.** These were tried, and each fails somewhere:
  - a low-rank separable fold (rms 0.12) and egg-crate hypotheses;
  - FM row scaling (good only for |Y| < 130);
  - an affine row warp (outer rows r ~0.9);
  - chirped per-row phase tracking (fails beyond |Y| ~120).

  The surface is therefore carried as measured fields over the folded half plane:
  - **F**, the shading: a weighted LSQ fit with the thin-line pixels masked. Its smoothing is
    100x stronger in the fin zone (right, |Y| > ~80), so the fins come out as continuous bands;
  - **S**, the signed distance to the photo's thin-line skeleton, fitted as its own spline (4x
    smoothing in the fin zone). A level set of F alone gave wobbly lines and spurious lines along
    steep soft edges;
  - **P**, line presence: it gates out the closures that S must make at soft edges.

  F, S and P are quadratic B-splines on 16 px x 24 line knots (28 x 122 knots, 8 + 7 + 1 bits).

## What it does
1. **Per field** (one serial multiplier, 3 slots): the K1 zoom zx, the field X of pixel 0 (x0,
   centred on the virtual width), and the knot-row step zk = zy / 24. K1, K2, K3, K5 and P12
   glide (1/8 of the gap per field). The breathing LFO is a triangle that is 0 at phase 0, so
   with K4 in the centre the picture is still and is the photo.
2. **Per line** (kicked at the source avid rise, computes the NEXT line, ~490 clocks, which fits
   SD's 858):
   - Y = |y - 460| (Twin: | |y - 460| - 270 |), then Yk = Y zk (knot row + 8-bit fraction),
     reflected at the bottom of the grid;
   - the B-spline weights b0 = (1 - v)^2 / 2, b2 = v^2 / 2, b1 = 1 - b0 - b2 (two products);
   - the Sway offset q (128 - q) of the row phase (one product);
   - the MAC: 123 knot columns x 3 rows, F, S and P in parallel. It writes 121 **sample words**
     into the next line's RAM bank. Word j holds F, S and P at X = 16 j and 16 j + 8, made from
     three consecutive columns by shift-adds ((c0 + c1) / 2 and (c0 + 6 c1 + c2) / 8);
   - in the blanking, the first three words are preloaded into the pixel window.
3. **Pixel path** (24 stages):
   - 0: field X DDA, segment-change flag;
   - 1: 3-word window (shift + refill), the 4-bit fraction, |dX|;
   - 2: the two samples around the pixel; octagonal distance to the sphere centre; the source
     value (camera luma - 512, or the Osc 2-D triangle);
   - 3-4: linear interpolation of F, S and P (one small product each); the dome (cone) and source
     products;
   - 5-6: F in Q.12 + dome + source + the breathing offset. S gets the same offset x 25/64, so the
     lines ride the shading;
   - 7-8: P12 gain (mantissa x octave shift) about the line level F = 0.30, and |S|;
   - 9: triangle fold (|G| mod 2) -> palette address t; the line level from (half-width - |S|);
     the notch flag (|S| shrinking just outside the line);
   - 10-12: presence and fold lines (P12 > 50 %), palette read, smoothstep;
   - 13: tail (fast attack, x0.78 decay per pixel), Neon;
   - 14-16: white blend (luma 5-bit, chroma 4 levels), notch (-1/32 or -1/16 luma);
   - 17-23: output conditioning ([1 2 1] luma, [1 2 2 2 2 2 2 2 1]/16 chroma, luma 13/16), then
     the blanking gate on the generated avid. Stage 24 is the output register.
4. **Palette** (generated by `chartreuse.py`):
   - Luma goes through a monotone tone curve, photo Y 44.6 / 100 / 165 / 210 / 245.5 -> drawn Y
     38 / 95 / 163 / 196 / 207 (after the 13/16 output scale, white ~777). The darks and mids stay
     at the photo's levels, slightly steeper through the mids for gloss, and only the top is
     compressed smoothly into the HDMI-bar ceiling.
   - Chroma is the photo's, scaled only to stay in the RGB gamut. The top of the Chartreuse ramp
     keeps at least Cb -22 / Cr -3 (8-bit), so the capped whites read pale lime-white, not grey.
5. **Memories** (23 EBR):
   - C_FSA 2048x16 (8), C_FSB 1024x16 (4), C_FSC 512x16 (2): the knot words;
   - C_PY / C_PU / C_PV 512x8 (3): two palettes, Chartreuse and Ember;
   - the sample-word RAM, 2 banks x 128 x 56 bits (4);
   - two 256x16 delay rings (2): chroma for the 8-tap box, and the syncs.

   All written RAMs carry no_rw_check. Tables are in hardware convention (u = Cr, v = Cb).

## Video
The camera's luma, minus mid-grey, is added to the surface level F (and, scaled, to the line
field S). The bands and their outlines bend around the picture: bright areas push the folds one
way and dark areas the other. The composition stays readable at the default Picture. Osc mode
feeds a slow 2-D triangle plaid through the same path.

## Engine notes
- **Contracts:** damier's generated avid; one 24-clock latency in every mode
  (processing_delay_clks 24); virtual 1080-line coordinates from the timing ID.
- **Fit history:**
  - v1 (quadratic evaluation per pixel, r^2 dome DDA, look-ahead notch) was 8,964 LC (116 %) and
    failed to place.
  - v2 (sample words + linear interpolation) was 5,544 LUT standalone and still too big.
  - v3 has a one-product engine, a cone dome, a local notch, a 4-level chroma blend and binary
    fold lines. It moves the syncs and the chroma box into EBR rings and cuts the latency from 32
    to 24. It packs to ~6,970 LC.
- router2 stalled (overused = 2) on HD Analog; router1 closes it. Rebuild with router1.

## Phase 2 (v0.6.0 2026-10-31, v0.6.1 2026-11-02)
Ron's notes on v0.5.2: K1 Crescents / K2 Arms should separate the shape into components that scale and
move differently instead of a global X / Y zoom, with more range both ways; K3 Sphere is subtle, widen it
both ways; the breathing should be much more prominent, with much more movement; S8 Sway is redundant
(Speed turns it off): repurpose it; Speed's full travel should be what the old -40 .. +40 % was.

What changed (control table above):
- **Field X map, per line.** The sphere zone (|X| < 640) keeps unit scale; beyond it the beam steps at the
  line's crescent spacing d(Y) = dce (1 + swing Y / 620), clamped to x0.15 .. x2.4 (x2.4 keeps the
  field inside one mirror period and the window refill >= 3 pixels apart at SD). The zone switch is
  taken from the previous pixel (one pixel late). The line engine computes d(Y) and, for Mirror, the
  field X of pixel 0 (-(640 + (vw/2 - 640 - sway) d) - 4) on the serial multiplier while the MAC runs.
- **K2's old vertical scale is gone**; the knot-row step is the photo's constant.
- **Dome** centred on the sphere (X 360, was 480), radius set by K3 (fixed slope 24 / px), round within
  3 % (r = max(mx, 7/8 mx + mn/2)); taken from |X| so it mirrors with S8 and, lying inside the sphere
  zone, stays round at any K1 / K2.
- **Breathing:** the triangle LFO's depth (3 x 0 .. 256) is linear in |K4 - centre|; the offset is
  +-768 Q.12 (old +-256); the same LFO pulses the crescent spacing (slot 8). The lines ride only the
  source now (not the breathing or the dome), so they hold their shape while the shading moves.
- **Sway** is always on, depth from |K4 - centre| (+-48 px at the ends, 0 at the centre).
- **Per-field slots** (one serial multiplier, 9 slots in the vblank): K1 spacing, K2 swing, K3 radius,
  K4 speed, K4 depth, K5 line width, LFO x depth, spacing x |offset|, pulsed spacing x swing.
- **Fit:** the first v0.6.0 build was 7,844 LC (102 %). Recovered by moving the knob curves into the
  slots, the dome from a multiplier to radius x 24, glides on K1 / K2 / P12 only (K3, K5 read the knob
  directly), the fold-line windows as bit decodes of t and G ([1152, 1344)), the fold-line gate straight
  from P12, the RAM output register as the window's third word, and 8-bit Osc drift phases (Osc x4,
  was x3.5).
- **v0.6.1 = v0.6.0 + the family-wide output-filter resync** (96c4a7c, applied here in the working tree):
  the chroma fed to the two running 8-sums is forced neutral off the generated avid, and both sums
  snap back to 8 x 128 once 9 blank samples are in, so one bad ring sample can no longer leave a
  permanent wrapped colour offset.
- A bug caught on the model sheet before flashing: K1's lower half ran backwards (x1 at 0, x0.35 at
  511) after the knob offsets became |k - 512|; the lower half is now 256 - product.
- Verified in GHDL (720p60, decimation 1) before the v0.6.1 resync: the default video frame matches
  v0.5.2 apart from the gentle default sway, and a K1 900 / K2 900 / K3 1000 / S8 / P12 800 Osc frame
  matches the integer model (`.heirloom_work/rb_chartreuse/p2/model060.py`) bit for bit, after the
  constant 4-px generated-avid offset.
- Defaults: K1 / K2 / K3 at the centre reproduce the v0.5.2 geometry; K4 at its default (640) moves
  gently (25 % depth: +-12 px sway, the old breathing size). Preset 0 "Photo" sets K4 to the centre: the
  still photo.

## Deviations from the photo, and why
- The brightest areas (the sphere, the white crescent) are dimmer and pale lime-cream rather than
  the photo's near-white. The HDMI-bar luma ceiling (drawn white ~777 = 8-bit 207, against the
  photo's 245) leaves no headroom at the top. The darks and mids keep the photo's levels; overall
  the picture is ~12 darker in RGB.
- The zig-zag fins (top and bottom right) are coarser than the photo: smooth continuous bands with
  rounded corners (16 x 24 px knots), not its sharp Z-corners. A few short line fragments remain at
  the extreme right; raising the presence threshold there removed real outline pixels nearly as
  fast as stray ones, so it was left. The photo's top and bottom fins are not mirror images, and
  the mirrored field carries one averaged pattern.
- The lines wobble a few pixels about the photo's. The notch before each line comes from |S|
  shrinking, not from a look-ahead of the line itself, and is thinner than the photo's.
- Resolution: the same picture at SD, 720p and 1080p (virtual coordinates). SD (4:3) shows the
  central 1440 virtual px, so the left part of the sphere and the right-most crescent are cropped.
