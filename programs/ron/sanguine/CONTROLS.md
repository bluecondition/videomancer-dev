# SANGUINE — Controls

Blood drips from the top edge: beads swell, break, fall with stuttering
gravity, and paint a permanent solid-red record of their path. Nothing fades
or darkens; more blood falls on top until S10 PURGE clears the canvas.

## Knobs

| Knob | Name | CCW | CW |
|------|------|-----|-----|
| K1 | **FLOW** | two or three lonely drips | dense curtain across the top edge |
| K2 | **VISCOSITY** | thick + syrupy: fat beads, long pauses, slow release | thin + runny: fast, narrow, quick-breaking |
| K3 | **HUE** | orange-red | violet-red (arterial crimson at 12 o'clock; expansion path for other liquids) |
| K4 | **DEPTH** | bright fresh crimson | deep dark venous red (luma + saturation shaped together) |
| K5 | **WANDER** | dead-straight runs | pronounced meander (per-lane grooves; repeat drips retrace the same path) |
| K6 | **SIZE** | hairline beads and trails (1 px base) | fat blobs (8 px base + mass, up to 15 px radius) |

## Slider

**P12 — HEMORRHAGE**: surge gesture. Push = burst of fresh rivulets, boosted
flow/speed/bead size, suppressed pauses (fast attack); past half the beads
skip surface tension and gush straight into the fall. Release = drains back
to the knob baseline (slow release). Playable in time with music.

## Switches

| Switch | Down | Up |
|--------|------|-----|
| S7 **SOURCE** | blood over solid black | blood (opaque) over the incoming video; video stays clean elsewhere |
| S8 **CLING** | drips fall as if on glass | drips ride the input's luma: bright content slows and pools them (live over black too) |
| S9 **AGE** | scene starts fresh | pre-painted: half the lanes already carry a run of random depth |
| S10 **PURGE** | — | ANY flip clears the painted canvas, keeps live drips (momentary-style) |
| S11 **SHEEN** | matte | gloss: specular pinpoint on drip heads + wet sheen stripe along live trails |
