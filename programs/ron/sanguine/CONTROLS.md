# SANGUINE — Controls

Blood drips from the top edge: beads swell, break, fall with stuttering
gravity, leave tapering wet trails that darken, dry, and stain.

The brief's 8 knobs are folded onto the 6 hardware knobs: **WANDER rides
VISCOSITY** (thick syrupy blood meanders and pauses; thin blood runs straight
and fast — one physical axis), and **GLINT is S11** (Matte/Gloss).

## Knobs

| Knob | Name | CCW | CW |
|------|------|-----|-----|
| K1 | **FLOW** | two or three lonely drips | dense curtain across the top edge |
| K2 | **VISCOSITY** | thick + syrupy: fat beads, wide trails, long pauses, pronounced meander | thin + runny: fast, narrow, straight, quick-breaking |
| K3 | **HUE** | orange-red | violet-red (arterial crimson at 12 o'clock; expansion path for other liquids) |
| K4 | **DEPTH** | bright fresh crimson | deep dark venous red (luma + saturation shaped together) |
| K5 | **TRAIL** | falling droplets only | continuous streaks to the bottom of frame |
| K6 | **COAGULATE** | trails fade shortly after the head passes | trails dry into permanent accumulating stains (mid = slow dry, gradual clear) |

## Slider

**P12 — HEMORRHAGE**: surge gesture. Push = burst of fresh rivulets, boosted
flow/speed/bead size, suppressed pauses (fast attack). Release = drains back
to the knob baseline (slow release). Playable in time with music.

## Switches

| Switch | Down | Up |
|--------|------|-----|
| S7 **SOURCE** | blood over solid black | blood (opaque) over the incoming video; video stays clean elsewhere |
| S8 **CLING** | drips fall as if on glass | drips ride the video's luma: bright content slows and pools them (video mode) |
| S9 **AGE** | scene starts fresh | pre-aged: dried runs already present under new drips |
| S10 **PURGE** | — | ANY flip clears stains + finished trails, keeps live drips (momentary-style) |
| S11 **SHEEN** | matte | gloss: specular pinpoint on drip heads + wet sheen along fresh trails |

## Presets

- **First Blood** — defaults, over black.
- **Slow Seep** — sparse, thick, syrupy, long trails, slow dry.
- **Crime Scene** — over video, cling on, pre-aged, permanent stains.
- **Arterial** — bright fast heavy flow, quick fade.
