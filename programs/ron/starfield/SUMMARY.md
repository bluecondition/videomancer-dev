# Starfield — glowing parallax starfield with living stars

## What it does
A deep-space starfield with up to 8 parallax depth layers, each scrolling
sideways at a different speed. Stars are soft glowing sprites in four
shapes — gaussian glow, soft square, diffraction spike, point+halo — with
smooth baked falloff (no brightness banding) and per-star size variety.
Stars twinkle at per-star phases and rates, and the population slowly
churns: stars fade in over seconds, live, and fade out (about a quarter
stay permanent). Star colours come from one of four palettes whose hues
shimmer slowly around their base tones. An optional Deep Space background
adds drifting nebula wisps under a subtle vertical gradient. A Luma Mode
maps the incoming video's brightness to local star density (Luma Gate
sharpens the mapping) and enlarges stars over bright video.

## How it works
Pure synthesis with optional video reactivity. Per-cell hashes (16x16 px
cells) generate deterministic star positions/properties; XOR + multiply +
xor-shift with per-layer seeds decorrelate the 8 layers. Star rendering is
a per-layer **glow-kernel ROM in EBR**: address {glow level(2b), shape(2b),
|dy|(3b), |dx|(3b)} → 8-bit weight, with per-layer depth fade baked into
the ROM contents (16 EBRs). Everything downstream of the hash runs in an
8-bit weight domain: a per-layer 8x5 envelope multiply applies the combined
twinkle×fade envelope, sum/max trees composite the layers (+ background
weight), and a single shared 10x10 multiplier applies P12 brightness at the
end. Colour is a shimmer-palette EBR (16-step hue orbits per palette
entry, baked offline). The background is a 64x64 tileable FBM nebula
texture in EBR, nearest-sampled at 4x stretch with a wrapping drift scroll
tied to 1/16 of the front-layer speed. Kernel/palette/nebula tables are
generated by `gen_kernels.py` → `starfield_rom_pkg.vhd`.

Pipeline LATENCY = 16. Knob-derived controls are pre-registered
(threshold_r pattern) and star property derivation is split across two
stages (s3p → s4a1) — both were needed to close HD timing at 74.25 MHz;
the register-file→property-cone route was the original critical path.
~7,170 LCs (93%), 24/32 EBRs, all 6 configs pass at full clock.

## Controls
- **K1 Layers** — 1..8 active parallax layers
- **K2 Density** — manual density (or luma gain in Luma Mode)
- **K3 Speed** — scroll speed (max ~38 px/frame; nebula drifts at 1/16)
- **K4 Palette** — Mixed / Cool / Warm / Rainbow, plus "+" (Lively)
  variants: deeper faster twinkle, ~3x faster fade churn, faster shimmer
- **K5 Glow** — kernel glow level with per-star dither (smooth growth)
- **K6 Depth** — layer parallax spread, 1..8 / Max
- **T7 Direction** — Left / Right scroll
- **T8 Color Mode** — Color / B&W
- **T9 Luma Mode** — Off / On (map video luma to star density and size)
- **T10 Background** — Off / Deep Space (nebula + gradient)
- **T11 Luma Gate** — Off / On (sharpen luma-to-density mapping)
- **Slider Brightness** — overall output brightness (true linear multiply)
