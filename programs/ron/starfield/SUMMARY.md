# Starfield — glowing parallax starfield with living stars

## What it does
A deep-space starfield with up to 8 parallax depth layers, each scrolling
sideways at a different speed. Stars are soft glowing sprites in four
shapes — gaussian glow, soft square, diffraction spike, point+halo — with
smooth baked falloff (no brightness banding) and per-star size variety.
Stars twinkle at per-star phases and rates, and the population slowly
churns: stars fade in over seconds, live, and fade out (about a quarter
stay permanent). Star colours come from eight palettes whose hues shimmer
slowly around their base tones. An optional Deep Space background adds
drifting nebula wisps under a subtle vertical gradient. Luma Mode maps the
incoming video's brightness to local star density and star size; Video
composites the stars over the input picture.

## How it works
Per-cell hashes generate deterministic star positions/properties — 16x16
px cells on the six far layers, 32x32 px cells on the near pair (layers
6–7, which also get a doubled density threshold: half the stars per area); XOR + multiply (canonical signed-digit shift-adds)
+ xor-shift with per-layer seeds decorrelate the 8 layers. Star rendering
is a per-layer **glow-kernel ROM in EBR**: address {glow level(2b),
shape(2b), |dy|, |dx|} → 8-bit weight, with per-layer depth fade baked
into the ROM contents. Kernels are **cell-safe** (the neighbouring cell
draws only its own star, so a kernel must be zero before its cell edge):
far layers use offsets 4..11 of 16 and kernels zero from 4 units, so |d| ≤
3 holds every weight (256-entry ROM, one EBR); the near pair uses offsets
8..23 of 32 and v3.0's full-size kernels (zero beyond 7.2, |d| ≤ 7,
1024-entry ROM, two EBR) — the size range runs from pinpoint dust to big
near stars. Everything downstream of the hash runs in an
8-bit weight domain: a per-layer 8x5 envelope multiply applies the combined
twinkle×fade envelope, sum/max trees composite the layers (+ background
weight), and a single shared 10x10 multiplier applies Brightness at the
end. Colour is a shimmer-palette EBR (16-step hue orbits per palette
entry, baked offline) with a per-entry luma dim. The background is a 64x64
tileable FBM nebula texture in EBR, nearest-sampled at 4x stretch with a
wrapping drift scroll at 1/16 of the front-layer speed. Scroll is 18.6
fixed point (sub-pixel speeds); the P12 speed curve is a 256-entry EBR
table. Video comes through a 16-deep EBR delay line (`no_rw_check`: the
pointers never collide) into an alpha-over composite. Kernel / palette /
dim / nebula / speed tables are generated by `gen_kernels.py` →
`starfield_rom_pkg.vhd`.

Pipeline LATENCY = 18. Knob-derived controls are pre-registered
(threshold_r pattern) and star property derivation is split across two
stages (s3p → s4a1) — both were needed to close HD timing at 74.25 MHz;
the register-file→property-cone route was the original critical path.

## Controls
- **K1 Layers** — 1..8 active parallax layers
- **K2 Density** — star density (in Luma Mode: the luma → density gain, 0..7.5×)
- **K3 Brightness** — output brightness (linear multiply; star cores cap at Y 768)
- **K4 Palette** — Silver, Candle, Twilight, Neon, Ember, Honey, Rainbow,
  B&W (warpfield's HW-approved set; per-entry luma dim on star pixels)
- **K5 Glow** — kernel glow level with per-star dither (smooth growth)
- **K6 Depth** — layer parallax spread, 1..7 / Max (far layers floor at
  1/16 of the front speed)
- **T7 Direction** — Left / Right scroll
- **T8 Video** — Off / On: stars composited OVER the input picture (alpha
  'over': alpha from the brightest star's kernel weight in 5 bands, out =
  video + (star − video)·a by shift-adds, no clamps). Deep Space = faint
  blue-violet cast on the video.
- **T9 Luma Mode** — video luma → star density AND size: luma − 512 is a
  signed, dithered glow-level offset on top of K5 (black ≈ −1.75 levels →
  pinpoints, white ≈ +1.7 → big glows)
- **T10 Background** — Off / Deep Space (nebula + gradient)
- **T11 Gate** — Luma Mode: steeper luma-to-density map ((luma−256)×2);
  manual mode: thins the field to the two bold magnitude classes
- **Slider Speed** — quadratic: a 0.25 px/frame drift at 0 (never stops,
  so Direction/Depth stay live), ~10 px/frame at 50 %, ~40 px/frame at
  full; the top half also switches to the Lively mood (deeper, faster
  twinkle, ~3× faster fade churn, faster shimmer)

## v3.1.0 (2026-10-10) — finalization pass (warpfield's lessons ported)
- `program_type` → `processing`: Luma Mode reads input luma, and
  `synthesis` routes no input (T9/T11 were dead on hardware).
- Labelled defaults: the firmware loads a labelled default as its label
  INDEX, so Layers "8" / Depth "4" opened as 1 layer / Depth 1. K1 and K6
  now take a register value below 8 as the label index (edgelab's fix).
- Cell-edge kernel clip (HW-confirmed on warpfield): offsets 2..13 →
  4..11, kernel window 2.5→4.0 with re-fitted sigmas, spike-offset clamp
  removed. Kernel ROMs 1024 → 256 entries: 16 → 8 EBR.
- Blanking gate: neutral black (Y 64, U=V 512) outside the latency-aligned
  avid.
- Saw-active vsync guard: scroll, twinkle, fade, shimmer and nebula step
  once per field under serrated analog vsync (were N× fast on analog).
- Levels: Y = 64 + product capped at 768 (the first build also scaled by
  11/16 — removed after HW feedback, below) (was 0-based black, peaks
  1019+ killed star chroma).
- Hash `× 0x9E37` in canonical signed-digit form (bit-identical).
- P12 = Speed (quadratic EBR curve, slow-drift floor, Lively on the top
  half); Brightness moved to K3; the K4 hidden mood bit is gone.
- Sub-pixel scroll (18.6 fixed point): v3.0 clamped every layer to ≥ 1
  px/frame, so at slow speeds all layers moved together (no parallax).
- 8 distinct palettes on K4 (B&W at position 8) + per-entry luma dim.
- K2 live in Luma Mode as the gain; T11 Gate has a manual-mode meaning.
- Luma size boost: up to ONE dithered level (was +2 whole levels).
- T8 Video: alpha-over composite from warpfield; video via a 16-deep EBR
  delay line with `no_rw_check` (0 transparency-emulation cells).

## v3.1.0 HW feedback (2026-10-10): size range too small
Ron: "the size range of the stars should be a little bigger, Starfield
doesn't look as good as it has in the past". Two causes: the cell-safe fix
capped every star at ~8 px across (v3.0 reached ~14; warpfield hid the loss
under its 1–2× zoom magnification, starfield has none), and the ×11/16
brightness pre-scale dimmed glow halos ~30%. Fixes: the near pair (layers
6–7) moved to 32 px cells with v3.0's exact kernel ROMs (bit-identical,
cell-safe there with offsets 8..23), and the ×11/16 pre-scale is gone —
the 768 cap alone keeps cores off full scale. +2 EBR.

## Luma Mode size, exaggerated (2026-10-11, Ron's HW request)
"Bigger size/glow in luma areas, and reduction of star size in darker
areas": the luma offset went from unsigned +0..+1 level to signed ±~1.75
levels about mid-grey, pre-added to the Glow base once per pixel (lbase_r)
so the per-layer level adder keeps its depth; the 8 per-layer lboost
registers are gone. Manual mode is bit-identical. Defaults captured from
hardware just before this change (Layers 6, Density/Brightness 100%,
Glow 524, Depth 5, Right, Speed 492).

## Community port — fit for the stock SDK (v3.1.2, 2026-10-14)
- The stock LZX SDK builds 8 configs, and its SD Standalone core is ~600 LC
  larger than HD. v3.1.1 packed to 8365/7680 LC there (HD Dual 7941).
- v3.1.2 serializes the per-field scroll step. Before, eight parallel copies of
  the deceleration ladder (`accum_decel_r`), `speed - ladder` (`diff_r`) and the
  floor clamp (`scroll_delta_r`) were recomputed every clock, plus eight 24-bit
  add/subtracts on `scroll`, all sampled once per field. Now the guarded field
  start runs one datapath for 8 clocks:
  - `sc_acc` builds (7 - i) * decel one add per clock, from layer 7 down to 0
    (the same additions in the same order, so the same 16-bit wrap);
  - then speed minus ladder, the floor clamp, and the add/subtract on
    `scroll(7)`, while the eight scroll registers rotate one place;
  - after 8 steps every layer is back in its own register.
  The rotation needs no 8:1 read mux. Scroll is only read by the pixel path,
  and the step finishes inside vertical blanking.
- Proven bit-exact against v3.1.1: GHDL side-by-side, 40 frames (403 x 201
  raster), knobs and switches changing mid-picture, 0 mismatches in 3.24 M clocks.
  (The old version sampled the knobs a few clocks before the field start, the
  new one a few clocks after, so a knob moving in exactly that window lands one
  field earlier or later.)
- Packed LC (stock SDK): SD Standalone 8365 → 7475, HD Dual 7941 → 7039.
