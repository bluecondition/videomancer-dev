# Tallow — melting rainbow columns

**v0.3.0 (2026-10-13): built, NOT flashed.** Ron asked for a switch that makes it more like
the original photo, whose column widths carry a variable noise. S11 is now **Noise**, the Bands
fold moved onto the Palette knob, and the Signal palette's mid-section greens are brighter
and purer.
**v0.2.0:** v0.1.0 drew well in preview (Ron: "pretty good"), but
its Saw wave's reset broke every edge along a horizontal seam. v0.2 replaces it with the seamless
Drip wave and caps Bend's depth. Made from a reference photo
(an analog-synth capture: rainbow and blue/green striped columns with dripping S-curve
edges, bent stripes and fringe lines). Presets are hand-written placeholders.

## What it does
It is an analog colourizer patch, rebuilt as one pass.
1. **Oscillator.** A horizontal oscillator is hard-synced to every line. A vertical
   LFO modulates its frequency: `f = fa0·(1 + depth·lfo(y))`. Because of the hard
   sync, the left columns stand still and the right ones swing. Every edge is a
   scaled copy of the LFO.
2. **Key.** The oscillator's triangle, compared against Width, splits the picture
   into rainbow columns (key 0) and stripe columns (key 1).
3. **Lag.** A one-pole exponential lag runs across the key. Its tail bends the
   stripes into each edge, and its fast sweep through the palette draws the thin
   fringe lines.
4. **Palette.** The index is `ramp(y) + lag`. Entries 0–255 are the rainbow ramp
   and 256–511 the stripe ramp. Entries n and n+1 are interpolated.

| Control | Function |
|---|---|
| K1 Columns | 1–32 columns across (square law) |
| K2 Width | Rainbow share of each column, 6–92 % |
| K3 Smear | Lag length, 1–512 px (exponential, dithered between powers of 2) |
| K4 Speed | LFO roll, bipolar. The centre ±24 counts is still; positive rolls the shapes down |
| K5 Bends | LFO cycles down the frame, 0.25–6 (square law) |
| K6 Palette | 8 positions: Signal (fitted to the reference) / Ember / Glacier / Acid, each Single or x3 (the ramp folded up-down-up) |
| S7 Wave | Drip = −cos of a skewed phase (rise over 80 % of the cycle, fall over 20 %): leaning edges that hook back, with no seam. Sine = smooth sway |
| S8 Twin | Second-harmonic key `(tri(φ) + tri(2φ+ψ))/2`: columns split into pairs; ψ drifts with Speed |
| S9 Jitter | Per-line start jitter, 0–7 px HD / 0–3 px SD (analog tearing) |
| S10 Anchor | Left / Centre (still point at mid-screen: `ph0 = −f·W/2`) |
| S11 Noise | Per-column width noise, default On. Each column thins and swells on its own, as in the photo: value noise over (column number, 6 cells per frame), smoothstep-interpolated down the frame, drifting with Speed, threshold ±192 (1.5×; at 2× the stripes broke into islands) |
| P12 Bend | FM depth 6–84 %. It never reaches 0, so Bends/Wave/Speed always show. It never reaches 100 % either: there f stalls at the wave's trough and a blank band opens. At full slider = a giant zigzag fan |

## Engine
- **Sequencer:** one serial shift-add multiplier and one serial restoring divider.
  - **Per field** (first vsync edge after active video): `2^24/W` and `2^20/H`, the
    knob curves, fa0, the FM amplitude, the LFO step, and speed.
  - **Per active line** (kicked by the generated avid's fall, ~35–48 clocks): the
    LFO (a parabolic sine `x(2048−|x|)` on the multiplier), f, and the
    centre-anchor phase.
- **Lag in one adder:** the target is all-ones or zero, so `key − Ks` is `~Ks`
  shifted with fill = not key, plus a carry-in. The shift is one-hot. An ordered
  x-dither picks s or s+1 per pixel, which makes Smear continuous.
- **Palette:** generated by `tallow.py` into `tallow_pkg.vhd`. There are 4 palettes
  of 512 entries, 8-bit Y/Cr/Cb (U/V-swapped), as six 1024×8 even/odd bank ROMs
  (12 EBR). Interpolation by the 4-bit index fraction gives 10-bit output.
- **Width noise (v0.3):** the phase accumulator carries 6 integer bits (the column number),
  so the noise rides the bending columns. Table: 32 cells × 64 columns, uniform and seeded
  (`tallow.py`), as two 1024×8 ROMs split by cell parity (4 EBR). Per line: the cell pair
  and a smoothstep weight. Per pixel: two ROM reads, a lerp, and a threshold offset. The
  key compare waits 4 stages for it, so latency is 19 stages in every mode.
- **Contracts:** damier's generated avid; 19-stage latency in every mode; the
  [1 2 1] Y/U/V smoothing + 13/16 luma bar fix; blanking gate; glides (±4
  deadband + ease) on K1/K2/K3/K5/P12.

## Design lessons
- **No seams from a reset LFO.** Under hard-sync FM, any jump in f moves every edge except the
  anchor at once. That draws a full-width horizontal break. To keep the saw character, skew the
  wave's phase instead of resetting it.
- **Don't take FM depth to 100 %.** A smooth wave dwells at its trough, and f ≈ 0 there gives
  columns of infinite width (a blank band of bare palette ramp).

## Build notes
- **v0.3.0 (2026-10-13), router2, all 6 configs on seed 1.** Fmax: HD Analog 86.0, HD HDMI 85.6,
  HD Dual 82.5, SD Analog 82.9, SD HDMI 86.9, SD Dual 94.6 MHz. ~5630 LC (73 %), 16/32 EBR,
  no transparency emulation. Every bitstream passed iceunpack.
- **v0.2.0 (2026-10-13), router2. Build seeds: HD HDMI = seed 2** (seed 1 missed timing); the
  other five are seed 1. Fmax: HD Analog 80.4, HD HDMI 86.1, HD Dual 89.7, SD Analog 87.8,
  SD HDMI 80.8, SD Dual 76.1 MHz. ~4985 LC (65 %), 12 EBR. Every bitstream passed iceunpack.
- **v0.1.0 (2026-10-13), router2, all 6 configs on seed 1.** Fmax: HD Analog 86.8,
  HD HDMI 84.2, HD Dual 82.7, SD Analog 86.4, SD HDMI 84.3, SD Dual 81.6 MHz.
  ~4920/7680 LC (64 %), 12/32 EBR, no transparency emulation. Every bitstream
  passed iceunpack.
- There is plenty of headroom (LC, EBR and ~10 % timing margin) for more palettes:
  8 × 512 entries would take 24 EBR, or more features.
