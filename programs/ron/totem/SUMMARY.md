# Totem — rippling curtains behind a stepped totem

**v0.1.0 (2026-10-13): built, NOT flashed.** Made from a reference capture: an analog-synth
picture of lit rainbow curtain bands that ripple more toward the bottom, a translucent diagonal
sheet, and a tapering column of alternating red slats in the middle. Presets are hand-written
placeholders.

## What it does
It is one colourizer patch drawn in a single pass.
1. **Curtains.** A band oscillator is synced to every line: `phase = tb + x·F`. A travelling
   wave made of two sines displaces it: `D = A(y)·(sin w1 + sin w2)`. Here `w1 = y·ky + x·kx`
   and `w2 = y·ky2 − x·kx2`, so the two waves run in opposite directions at 5/4 and 13/8 of the
   first wave's frequencies. Their beating gives irregular S-bends. The amplitude `A(y)` follows
   q² down the frame (q = line/H), so the top stays straight. Above half Ripple it opens toward q,
   and the ripple climbs the frame.
2. **Bands.** The integer part of the phase picks a palette row, and the fraction picks the place
   along it. Each band is a lit tube: dark and saturated at its left edge, peaking 71 % of the way
   across, then falling slightly into the next band's hard edge.
3. **Sheet.** Diagonal strips (`x·sx + y·sy`) switch alternate strips to the SHEET rows, which
   are the curtain rows turned −54° and made more saturated.
4. **Totem.** Inside `|x − c| < hw` the phase is the lens coordinate `tI − |x − c|·k(y)`, with
   `k = F·hw_top / hw(y)`. Each ribbon keeps its place across the column, so the ribbons
   converge as the column tapers. The column is cut into slats: even slats read the LENS rows
   (red ribbons and pastel swirls), odd slats read the flat FLAME rows. With Stepped edges, each
   slat keeps the width it had at its first line, and odd slats are inset by 3/16. The wave
   reaches into the column at 1/2 strength on even slats and 1/8 on odd slats.

| Control | Function |
|---|---|
| K1 Bands | 3–40 curtain bands across (square law) |
| K2 Width | Totem width at the top, 8–92 % of the screen |
| K3 Taper | Bipolar. Left = vase (flares downward, capped at the screen), centre ±16 = pillar, right = funnel (bottom down to 15 % of the top) |
| K4 Slats | 2–24 slats down the frame (square law) |
| K5 Speed | Bipolar, still in the centre ±24. The two waves travel in opposite directions, the bands drift, the totem's ribbons flow and the sheet slides |
| K6 Palette | Signal (fitted to the reference) / Ember (Signal turned 180°: warm curtains, icy totem) / Neon / Dusk |
| S7 Sheet | Diagonal tint strips Off / On |
| S8 Mirror | Totem ribbons mirrored about the centre Off / On |
| S9 Edges | Stepped slats (odd slats inset) / Smooth taper |
| S10 Swell | Long waves (2.5 down, 0.7 across) / Short waves (6 down, 1.6 across) |
| S11 Tear | Per-line start jitter of 0–3 px on HD (0–1 on SD) Off / On |
| P12 Ripple | Wave amplitude at the bottom, 1–37 % of the width. Past half way the ripple climbs up the frame. At 100 % the lower half folds into swirls and the totem buckles |

## Engine
- **Sequencer:** one serial shift-add multiplier and one general serial restoring divider (32
  quotient bits).
  - **Per field** (first vsync edge after active video): `2^24/W`, `2^20/H`, the knob curves,
    F, the column top and bottom half-widths and the per-line width step, the slat step,
    `Kin = F·hw_top`, the ripple amplitude in band phase, the wave and sheet steps, speed, and
    the time phases.
  - **Per active line** (kicked at the generated avid's start, computing the NEXT line, about
    110 clocks): the DDAs, q and q², the amplitude profile, the slat latch, `k = Kin / hw`
    (divider), the lens start phase `−c·k`, and the column edges.
- **Pixel path (18 stages):** six accumulators (x, band phase, lens phase, two wave phases,
  sheet). Two quarter-wave sine ROMs (1 EBR each) are summed. The magnitude is multiplied by A
  with its operand split lo/hi, then the signed displacement is added. The row and ramp position
  r·7/256 come next, then the even/odd palette bank ROMs and the 4-bit knot interpolation.
- **Palette:** generated by `totem.py` into `totem_pkg.vhd`. There are 4 palettes × 4 groups
  (curtain, sheet, lens, flame) × 16 rows × 8 knots, as six 1024×8 bank ROMs (12 EBR). The
  interpolation never crosses a row, so band edges stay hard.
- **Contracts:** damier's generated avid; one 18-stage latency in every mode; the [1 2 1] Y/U/V
  smoothing and 13/16 luma bar fix; the blanking gate; glides (±4 deadband + ease) on
  K1–K4 and P12; every DDA starts half a line down on the bottom field.

## Build notes
- **v0.1.0 (2026-10-13), router2. Build seeds: HD Analog = seed 2** (seed 1 stalled the
  router); the other five are seed 1. Fmax: HD Analog 75.9, HD HDMI 74.8, HD Dual 78.7,
  SD Analog 71.9, SD HDMI 83.1, SD Dual 72.7 MHz. ~6365/7680 LC (83 %), 14/32 EBR (12 palette
  + 2 sine). Every bitstream passed iceunpack. These are thin HD margins, built while five
  other tabs were building at the same time.
- **The limiter is the sequencer, not the pixel path.** The first build had ~25 states each
  writing the multiplier operands, some with inline adds. Its q_st → operand-mux/CEN cone
  failed 4 of 6 HD seeds.
  - The engine now writes only a 5-bit op code and go. The multiplier fetches its own
    operands into registers, then loads them a clock later.
  - The n_* hand-over layer is gone: the pixel path loads the engine's l_* registers at g_go.
  - After the accumulators, the phase is carried as bits 27..14 only, and the mirror is a
    one's complement.
  - These changes saved ~350 LC.
- If HD timing ever regresses, try a seed sweep first, then router1, then remove logic. At
  83 % LC, extra pipeline registers are unlikely to help.
