# Phyllo (Vellum)

**v0.1.0 built 2026-11-16, not flashed.** Flash `out/rev_b/ron/vellum/phyllo.vmprog`.

## What it draws

Phyllotaxis: a sunflower head, a pine cone, Vogel's model. Dots on a log-polar lattice filling the screen to
the corners. In (log r, theta) the lattice is generated by (eps, 2 pi alpha) and (0, 2 pi), so every dot is a
circle whose size grows with its distance from the centre, and the dots line up along two families of
exponential spirals (13 and 21 at the golden angle). The tiny dots at the centre dissolve into flat grey.
Grey: the dots step through four greys along one spiral family (grey spirals). Ink: white dots on black.
The default frame is the sunflower: golden angle, about 67 dots per octave, dots at 3/4 of touching, drifting
slowly outward.

    lattice coords   a = lam1 L + mu1 T + oa,  b = lam2 L + mu2 T + ob     (L = log2 r, T = theta / turn)
                     mu1, mu2 = the integers +-q of the reduced basis -> the theta wrap is seamless
    nearest point    j = round b, x = a + g fb, X = frac x, Y = h fb, d^2 = X^2 + Y^2   (row j only:
                     the dot radius never exceeds half the row spacing)
    Bold             coverage = clamp(1/2 - (d^2 - R^2) 2^sigma),  sigma = log2 r + C (the pixel size)
    Fine             a ring of constant area: |d^2 - Rm^2| - band
    Lines            the nearer lattice line of the two families, minus the half width
    Grey             dots x (k mod 4 + 1) / 4, k = i q1 + j q2 the point's label; lines: one family white,
                     the other half grey.  Ink: white.  Everything fades to its mean grey under ~2 px.

## Controls

| Control | Name | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | X | the centre, one screen width either way (a +-13-count detent at the middle); off-centre shows the sweep of the arms |
| K2 | Y | the same, vertically |
| K3 | Scale | density, exponential: 16 .. 256 dots per octave of radius (default 532 = 67) |
| K4 | Dot | dot radius 0.12 .. 1.0 of the base radius (pinpoints to touching: at the top the gaps form a negative lattice of curved stars) |
| K5 | Drift | bipolar growth: +-24 counts still, 2^-12 .. 2^-3 lattice cycle per field, exponential (the Hypnos speed path); the dots stream outward (or inward) along the most radial lattice direction, wrapping exactly at one lattice step; default 660 (+29 %) |
| K6 | Camera | the box-filtered luma swells the dot area (or widens the lines); default 100 % |
| S7 | Glide | Off / On (default On): K3, K4, K6, P12 ease 1/16 of the gap per field, K5 1/8, the centre 1/16 (at least 1 px, landing exactly); a +-1-count hold against pot jitter (ends latch). Off = no easing |
| S8 | Form | Dots / Lines (the exact spiral net: the two lattice-line families) |
| S9 | Tone | Grey (four greys by label) / Ink (white) |
| S10 | Weight | Bold (filled dots) / Fine (outlines of constant area) |
| S11 | Source | Video / Osc (the shell's drifting triangle lattice, peaks only, at 1.5 x) |
| P12 | ANGLE | the divergence alpha: 0 % = 4/11 (11 spokes), 50 % = golden 0.381966, 100 % = 2/5 (5 spokes); on the way the spirals straighten into spokes at 13 % (7/19, 19 spokes), 31 % (3/8, 8 spokes), 47 % (8/21), 57 % (5/13, 13 spokes), 69 % (7/18). Piecewise linear in alpha, the reduced basis recomputed every field |

Presets: Sunflower (default), Eight Spokes (Ink, P12 31 %), Pinwheel (Ink, Dot 84 %, P12 100 %),
Spiral Net (Lines, Ink, Scale 32 %), Seed Head (Ink, Scale 68 %, Dot 100 %), Pearl Rings (Fine, Scale 41 %, P12 55 %).

## Motion

The lattice is invariant under a shift of one lattice step, so the drift is two per-field accumulators (kappa
along log r, nu along theta, Q22 mod 4 cells: the labels stay valid) added to the forms as the offsets oa, ob.
The step goes along the most radial of w1, w2 and the short diagonal (outward for K5 > 512): dots stream out
of the centre along the spirals, swelling as they go. The speed is the Hypnos decode (2^(i/128) mantissa, an
octave shift): 2^-12 .. 2^-3 cycle per field over the knob, still within +-24 counts of the centre.

## Camera

Never shown: it swells the dots. Luma (or Osc) -> box means over blocks of 64 px (1080) / 32 px (720p, SD)
-> a vertical one-pole IIR per block (block RAM, shift 5 / 4 / 3 by mode) -> linear interpolation between
neighbouring blocks (a DDA, exact at every block start) -> x the per-field gain (K6 x the base radius^2) ->
added to R^2 (clamped to the half row spacing), or to the line half width. K6 0 % or a black input is exactly
the clean figure; at 100 % bright areas swell to touching and dark areas keep the K4 dots.

## Engine

- **Per field**: a microcoded bit-serial engine (program + bit-exact interpreter: ucode.py; 502 words of
  512, 101 registers + the 2^(i/128) table in one register-file block RAM). Bit-serial datapath (a, b, r
  shift registers, one full adder), a 32-cell serial-parallel multiplier, NORM by an msb scan, Newton
  reciprocals for the two divisions. It does the knob glides, alpha, E, the Gram matrix of the stored basis,
  the Lagrange-Gauss reduction (Gram updates, adds only), g and h, the forms, the drift, the dot radius, the
  AA / fade constants and the centre glide in about 12k clocks (it runs in blanking only and pauses in active
  video). Started at every field tick (the vsync edge after active video).
- **Front** (Hypnos log-polar tables) once per four pixels: one log2 unit takes hi and lo on two clocks,
  one slope multiplier g(u) and atan(u) on two clocks.
- **Samples** at pixels 0, 4, 8, ... (and one past the line): a, b, log2 r on one shared multiplier (four
  products per sample); the constants come from a block-RAM copy of engine registers R0..R3.
- **Per pixel**: a DDA between samples (the theta seam taken out of the step), g fb (12 x 10), h fb (13 x 9,
  fb shifted by h's exponent), squares by table + cross term, one AA ramp with a two-step shift, grey / fade,
  luma [1 2 1]/4, 13/16 toward black (white 776), blanking gate. U = V = 512.
- One latency (52) in every mode; generated avid with the parity-locked start; virtual 1080-line
  coordinates; 21 EBR.

## Cut / deviations

- K6 swells the dots' area (a halftone reading of the picture) instead of adding luma to the lattice
  phase as the spec sketched; the lattice itself stays exact.
- No two-row search: the dot radius is clamped to half the row spacing, so the nearest dot is in row j.
- Fitting from 11.5k LC: bit-serial engine (was a 32-bit parallel one), knob glides in microcode, the front
  at the sample rate, the camera as block means + per-block IIR (was a sliding box + per-column IIR),
  narrower g fb / h fb and lam (Q9), the multiplier constants from block RAM.
- NTSC: a knob jump that makes the reduction loop long (up to ~45k clocks) can run past the vblank into
  active-line blanking; the per-pixel constants then change mid-field for that one field.
- NTSC (720 px): the last half block of the camera field is never written, so the camera fades to zero over
  the right-most 16 px.
